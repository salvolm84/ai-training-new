Internal seminar series

AI Foundations

Twenty-one interactive modules that build from a single artificial neuron to a pre-qualified list of use cases from your own work. Written for engineers who are new to machine learning: every concept has something you can move, break and put back together, and every number in the calculators is computed rather than illustrated.

Running the series

Tracks, format and practicalities.

Suggested tracks

The full series is about seventeen hours, which is a ten- to twelve-week programme of weekly sessions. These shorter paths work as stand-alone deliveries.

Half-day executive

1 · 4 · 5 · 10 · 14

What it is, how the language models work, what it costs, what the law requires, and what is genuinely uncertain. Skip the mathematics entirely; keep the RAG simulator and the training calculator.

One day, engineers

2 · 3 · 6 · 7 · 11

The mechanism, the right algorithm for the data, the data itself, and the engineering applications. The most technically satisfying path and the one that generates the best use cases.

One day, delivery teams

7 · 8 · 9 · 13 · 21

Data, evaluation, agents, human factors, capstone. For people who will actually build or own something. Assumes Part I was delivered earlier.

Two hours, everyone

1 · 12 · 13.1

Vocabulary, the prompting workshop, and the automation-bias experiment. The broadest-appeal combination, and it satisfies the AI-literacy obligation discussed in module 10.

The narrative spine

2 → 3 → 4 → 8 → 13

If you only have one thread: the XOR failure in 2 is paid off in 3; the next-token objective in 4 explains the failures; 8 makes them measurable; 13 shows why people accept them anyway.

One day, shipping to the vehicle

15 · 16 · 19 · 20

Telemetry, honest uncertainty, running a model in production, and the safety case. For teams whose model will end up on a test bench, in the plant or on the car. Assumes modules 7 and 8.

Before each delivery

check 5, 10 and 20

Module 5's energy and cost figures, module 10's regulatory dates and module 20's standards status all move quickly, and all are sourced on the page. Ten minutes of checking protects your credibility.

How to run a session

Each module is one page you project directly from a browser — no internet connection required. Work top to bottom and stop at every Lab block to let people try it themselves; the labs are the module, and the prose is the handout. Press N for speaker notes, which include timing, the questions to expect, and where to slow down.

Keyboard

N speaker notes T light / dark ← → previous / next module

Speaker notes are hidden by default, so the same files can be handed to attendees. They do appear when a page is printed to PDF.

Honesty about the material

Every calculator computes its numbers live — the networks really train, the surrogates really fit, the statistics are really computed. Three things are authored rather than live and are labelled as such on the page: the tokenizer in module 4.1, the next-token probabilities in 4.3, and the attention maps in 4.4. Module 5's figures are sourced and dated; modules 10 and 20 are an engineer's orientation and not legal advice.

The thread that holds the whole series together is “it is all weighted sums and one loop”. Say it in module 2 and call back to it in module 4 and module 14 — people remember the callback, and it is the antidote to both credulity and mystification.

  • Module 2 ends on a failure (XOR). Leave the room slightly stuck; module 3 opens by rescuing them. Do not spoil it.
  • Module 4.8's RAG demo and module 9.3's poisoned document are the two most persuasive things in the series for a management audience. Budget time for both.
  • Module 13.1 works only if you do not warn them. Resist explaining first.
  • Module 7's readiness checklist and module 21's canvas are the two artefacts worth collecting. Together they are the most accurate picture of AI readiness anyone in the organisation will produce this year.
  • Record attendance. Under the AI-literacy obligation discussed in module 10.9, this series is a compliance measure as well as a training one.
